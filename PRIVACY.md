# Privacy Statement

**App:** WGRALGO Guardians of Health &amp; Recovery
**Publisher:** WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm&trade; Inc.
**Version:** 2.0.0

## What the app does

The app is an offline educational health-and-recovery quiz. All questions, answers, scores, and feedback are stored only in the app's local memory while you play. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## What the app does **not** do

- No account required
- No ads
- No analytics
- No trackers
- No data selling
- No cloud upload
- No personal health information is collected
- No quiz answers or scores are sent to WGRALGO
- No advertising or marketing identifiers used
- No third-party SDKs that phone home

## Android permissions

The app declares **no** dangerous permissions and **no** `INTERNET` permission (it is stripped from the final manifest). It cannot make network requests.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

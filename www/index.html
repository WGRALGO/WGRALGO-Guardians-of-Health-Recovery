How It Works

Round-based educational practice.

  1. 10 randomized questions per round.
  2. Categories: CASAC Practice, Ethics, Confidentiality, Pharmacology / Health Awareness.
  3. Tap an answer. You will see if you were correct or incorrect, the correct answer, an explanation, and a short learning tip.
  4. Your score is shown out of 10. The round complete screen shows strengths and weak spots by category.
  5. Replay anytime — questions reshuffle each round.

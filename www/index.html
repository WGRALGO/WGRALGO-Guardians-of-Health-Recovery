About

Guardians of Health & Recovery is a free health, safety, and recovery quiz from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Learn how alcohol and drugs affect the body, how to respond in an emergency, and how to support recovery.

How it works

  • Pick a level: All Levels, Beginner, Everyday, or Helper.
  • Answer 10 random questions from a bank of 90. Every answer explains why, and common myths get a 💡 myth buster.
  • The results show how you did by topic, with a review of every question.

Need help now?

  • Emergency or overdose: call 911
  • Mental health or suicide crisis: call or text 988
  • SAMHSA National Helpline (free, confidential, 24/7): 1-800-662-4357
